FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Where do sessions take place?
–
Sessions are available both online via Zoom and in-person across Sydney's Inner West and surrounding areas. We'll discuss the best option when you enquire.
How long are sessions and how often should we meet?
+
Standard sessions are 60 minutes. For most students, once a week is sufficient — though students approaching the HSC often benefit from twice weekly in Term 3 and 4.
Do you offer a free initial consultation?
+
Yes — the first 20-minute consultation is completely free. It's a chance to discuss your child's goals, current level, and what they need from tutoring.
Which subjects and year levels do you cover?
+
I specialise in English across Years 7–12, covering Standard English, Advanced English, and English Extension 1. I'm well-versed in all current HSC modules and the NSW NESA syllabus.
How do I pay, and are sessions charged in advance?
+
Invoices are issued weekly or fortnightly by preference. Payment is accepted via bank transfer. A cancellation policy applies — please give at least 24 hours' notice to avoid a cancellation fee.
Do you offer group sessions?
+
Small group sessions (2–4 students) are available at a reduced per-student rate. These work particularly well for HSC preparation workshops. Reach out to discuss availability.
